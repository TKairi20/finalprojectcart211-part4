07 / Data cycle

THE DATA
CYCLE.

Behavioural data can move through a repeating process in which predictions can be made by shaping what people see and do next.

➜ ➜ ➜ ➜
BEHAVIOURAL DATA
CYCLE
FIRST STAGE

COLLECT

Clicks, searches, views and other actions create signals.

SECOND STAGE

ANALYZE

Signals are organized to identify patterns and possible interests.

THIRD STAGE

PREDICT

Patterns can be used to predict what someone may do next.

FOURTH STAGE

INFLUENCE

Recommendations and targeted content may shape future actions.

FIFTH STAGE - IT RESTARTS AGAIN.

New actions will create new signals, which will then continue the cycle.
