09 / The economy of prediction

WHO BENEFITS
FROM YOUR DATA?

Behavioural data can be valuable because it helps companies to understand, predict and potentially influence what people might do in the future.

PLATFORMS

They can use behavioural information to personalize their services, improve the engagement and support their business models.

ADVERTISERS

The can pay to reach audiences they believe are more likely to be interested in a product or service.

USERS

They may receive recommendations and personalized experiences, while also giving up information about their behaviour.

A question to consider

IF YOUR BEHAVIOUR CREATES VALUE, WHO SHOULD CONTROL THAT VALUE?

Who collects the information? Who analyzes it? Who makes predictions? And who earns money from those predictions? Is it a good idea to give up those data?
