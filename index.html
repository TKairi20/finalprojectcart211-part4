HOW MUCH CAN THE INTERNET KNOW ABOUT YOU?

We use the internet every day to do all kinds of activities, such as searching for information or watching videos for example. By doing simple activities, we can reveal a lot of information about ourselves concerning our habits and interests.

This experiment will help you discover how your actions online can be used to make predictions.
